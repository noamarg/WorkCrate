# MOD-10 - Delivery packs

**Module ID:** MOD-10  
**Catalog toggle:** Yes  
**Sub-packs:** `engineering`, `design`, `marketing`  
**Status:** draft

## At a glance

Optional job-specific delivery on top of Work and Projects. Enable only the sub-packs your org needs.

## Sub-packs

| Sub-pack | Adds |
|----------|------|
| **engineering** | Backlog, sprints, bugs, releases, dev tool links |
| **design** | Creative requests, review rounds, asset library |
| **marketing** | Campaigns, content calendar, creative handoff |
