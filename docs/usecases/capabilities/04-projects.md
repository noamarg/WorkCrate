# MOD-04 - Projects

**Module ID:** MOD-04  
**Catalog toggle:** Yes  
**Status:** draft

## At a glance

- Project lifecycle: create, archive, templates, clone.
- Portfolios and programs; milestones and phases.
- Client-visible projects and guest portals.

## Problems it solves

Groups work under outcomes and timelines; separates internal delivery from what clients may see.

## Who uses it

| Persona | Typical need |
|---------|----------------|
| Product and delivery lead | Run projects and milestones |
| Agency owner | Client portals |
| Consultant | Per-engagement projects |

## Admin configuration

- **Typical profiles:** `Delivery`, `ClientGuest` (portal only).
