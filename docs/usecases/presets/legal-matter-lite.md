# Preset - Legal matter tracking (lite)

**Type:** Industry preset  
**Status:** draft

## Summary

Law firms and legal ops tracking matters as projects, deadlines as work items, and approval workflows-not full DMS.

## Recommended modules

| Module | Sub-packs |
|--------|-----------|
| MOD-04 Projects | matter = project |
| MOD-05 Work | deadlines, tasks |
| MOD-06 Workflows | ethics walls via permissions |
| MOD-09 Knowledge | matter notes |
| MOD-11 Revenue | client accounts |

## Experience profiles

| Profile | Audience |
|---------|----------|
| `Attorney` | Matters and work |
| `Paralegal` | Documents + work |
| `ClientGuest` | Portal per matter |
